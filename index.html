✨ How to Use This Generator

Use this tool to build a graffiti-style prompt, then test it in ChatGPT and/or Gemini to see which image result you like best.

Step 1: Pick your dropdown options for style, surface, paint effects, lighting, signature style, and color blend.
Step 2: Add extra direction in the custom text boxes to tell the AI exactly what vibe, brand name, or details you want.
Step 3: If you don’t know what to type in the custom boxes, go to ChatGPT and ask for ideas for your creative direction and must-have elements.
Step 4: Click Generate Prompt, then copy the finished prompt.
Step 5: Paste the prompt into ChatGPT and/or Gemini to generate your images.
Step 6: Compare both results and keep the one you like best. One might give you cleaner text, while the other might give you a crazier street-art vibe.
Quick tip: Put your brand name, color vibe, mood, and must-have details in the custom boxes. Example: royal blue and gold graffiti wall, luxury streetwear energy, chrome shine, bold brand name, upscale urban campaign look.
